Our Story

Rooted in Tradition

Established in 2012 in a small brick alleyway, L'Aura began as a humble micro-roastery. Our obsession with sourcing only the top 1% of specialty grade Arabica beans quickly drew a devoted following.

Over the years, we've expanded our footprint, yet our core philosophy remains unchanged. We roast in small batches, build direct relationships with farmers, and continually push the boundaries of modern coffee culture.
